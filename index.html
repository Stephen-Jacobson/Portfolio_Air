Tetris RL Agent

I built this Tetris engine from scratch to match the mechanics of tetris.com, including piece rotation with wall kicks, hold, a next piece queue, and scoring. It also works as a full training environment for a genetic algorithm agent.

The agent doesn't follow hand written rules. Its neural network was evolved over generations instead. For every legal placement of the falling piece, every rotation and every column, it simulates the resulting board, scores each outcome, and plays the best one. Each generation, the top performers are bred through weighted crossover and mutated weights, with elitism and checkpointing preserving progress along the way.

On a strong run it scores over 450,000 points, roughly 5x to 9x a typical casual player's average of 50,000 to 100,000.

This is a passion project I proposed and have been working on with my friends.

We are currently developing a Drone Crop-Watering model, Drone Navigation to a Point model, and a Drone Surveying Complex Environment model.

Drone Crop-Watering

A continuous-control reinforcement learning agent, built with a couple of friends, that flies a drone through a procedurally generated 3D voxel farm, avoiding terrain and trees while watering the crop regions that need it. We built the whole stack ourselves: a custom voxel terrain generator, a vectorised lidar-style raycasting engine for obstacle sensing and spray coverage, a region-completion tracker, and an Open3D live viewer for watching it fly.

The policy is trained with PPO (via TorchRL) on a 5-dimensional continuous action: 3D movement direction, a spray on/off toggle, and a learned move-speed scale. The drone is handed one needs-water region at a time, always the nearest unfinished one, with reward shaping and a staged difficulty curriculum scoped to whichever region is currently active.
